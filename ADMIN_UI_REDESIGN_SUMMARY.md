## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Emerald (500-600) & Cyan (500-600)
- **Sidebar**: Slate (900/800/700)
- **Success**: Green (500-600)
- **Warning**: Orange (500-600)
- **Danger**: Red (500-600)
- **Info**: Blue (500-600)
- **Accent**: Purple (500-600)

### Typography
- **Headings**: Bold, 2xl-3xl
- **Body**: Regular, sm-base
- **Labels**: Medium, xs-sm uppercase

### Spacing
- **Cards**: p-6
- **Sections**: mb-6, mb-8
- **Grid gaps**: gap-4, gap-6

### Borders & Shadows
- **Borders**: border-gray-100, border-gray-200
- **Shadows**: shadow-sm, shadow-lg, shadow-xl
- **Rounded**: rounded-xl, rounded-2xl

### Animations
- **Transitions**: transition-all duration-200/300
- **Hover**: hover:shadow-xl, hover:scale-105
- **Loading**: animate-spin, animate-pulse
