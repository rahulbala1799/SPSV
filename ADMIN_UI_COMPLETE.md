## 🎨 **Design System**

### **Color Palette**
```css
Primary: emerald-500 to cyan-500 (gradients)
Backgrounds: white, gray-50
Borders: gray-100, gray-200
Text: gray-900 (headings), gray-600 (body)
Success: emerald-500
Warning: amber-500
Error: red-500
Info: blue-500
```

### **Typography**
- **Headings**: text-3xl, font-bold, text-gray-900
- **Subheadings**: text-xl, font-bold, text-gray-900
- **Body**: text-base, text-gray-600
- **Labels**: text-sm, font-medium, text-gray-700

### **Spacing & Borders**
- **Cards**: rounded-2xl, shadow-sm, border border-gray-100
- **Buttons**: rounded-xl, px-6 py-3
- **Inputs**: rounded-xl, border-2, focus:border-emerald-500
- **Padding**: p-6 (cards), p-8 (pages)

### **Interactive Elements**
- **Buttons**: Gradient backgrounds, hover shadows, transitions
- **Cards**: Hover lift effect (shadow-lg on hover)
- **Inputs**: Focus rings with emerald color
- **Status Badges**: Color-coded, rounded-full
