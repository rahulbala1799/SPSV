## Chapter 1: Welcome (Pages 7-7)

### Question 1
**What does SPSV stand for?**
- A) Special Public Service Vehicle
- B) Small Public Service Vehicle
- C) Standard Public Service Vehicle
- D) State Public Service Vehicle
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: SPSV is short for 'small public service vehicle' – vehicles that are used to carry up to eight passengers, excluding the driver, for hire or payment.

### Question 2
**How many categories of SPSV are there?**
- A) 4
- B) 5
- C) 6
- D) 7
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The term SPSV includes: Taxis, Wheelchair accessible taxis, Hackneys, Wheelchair accessible hackneys, Local area hackneys, and Limousines.

### Question 3
**What is the maximum passenger capacity (excluding driver) for an SPSV?**
- A) 6 passengers
- B) 8 passengers
- C) 10 passengers
- D) 12 passengers
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: SPSVs are vehicles that are used to carry up to eight passengers, excluding the driver.

### Question 4
**Who created the Official Manual for Operating in the SPSV Industry?**
- A) Department of Transport
- B) National Transport Authority (NTA)
- C) An Garda Síochána
- D) Road Safety Authority
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The Manual has been created by the National Transport Authority (NTA) to give SPSV operators the information they need to apply for and maintain licences.

## Chapter 2: Terminology (Pages 8-12)

### Question 1
**What does 'adult size' refer to in terms of seatbelt usage?**
- A) Individuals over 140cm and weighing 30kg or more
- B) Individuals over 150cm and weighing 36kg or more
- C) Individuals over 160cm and weighing 40kg or more
- D) Individuals over 170cm and weighing 50kg or more
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: For seatbelt usage in a vehicle, 'adult size' refers to individuals over 150 centimetres and weighing 36 kilograms or more.

### Question 2
**What does the initial charge on a taximeter cover?**
- A) First 300 meters or 60 seconds
- B) First 400 meters or 75 seconds
- C) First 500 meters or 85 seconds
- D) First 600 meters or 90 seconds
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: When a journey begins, the initial (starting) fare charge is already on the taximeter. This covers the first 500 meters of a journey or a period of 85 seconds.

### Question 3
**What does Tariff A cover?**
- A) Next 10 km or 30 minutes
- B) Next 12 km or 35 minutes
- C) Next 14.5 km or 42 minutes
- D) Next 16 km or 45 minutes
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Tariff A is the standard fare charge that applies after the initial charge. This covers the next 14.5 km or 42 minutes of a journey.

### Question 4
**What is a 'dispatch operator'?**
- A) A driver who operates their own SPSV
- B) An individual or organisation that provides a booking service for SPSV journeys
- C) A company that owns multiple SPSVs
- D) A government agency that regulates SPSVs
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: An SPSV dispatch operator is an individual or organisation that provides a booking service/facility for SPSV journeys that are delivered by someone else.

## Chapter 3: The SPSV Industry (Pages 13-25)

### Question 1
**When was the National Transport Authority (NTA) established?**
- A) 1 December 2007
- B) 1 December 2009
- C) 1 December 2011
- D) 1 December 2013
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Established by the Minister for Transport on 1 December 2009, the NTA is a statutory non-commercial body.

### Question 2
**Which organization became part of NTA in 2011?**
- A) Road Safety Authority
- B) Commission for Taxi Regulation
- C) Department of Transport
- D) Local Authorities
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The NTA includes the previous regulatory organisation, the Commission for Taxi Regulation, which became part of NTA in 2011.

### Question 3
**How many main areas of licensing are there in the SPSV industry?**
- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 4
- D) 5
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: There are three main areas of licensing: Driver licences (provided by An Garda Síochána), Vehicle licences (provided by NTA), and Dispatch operator licences (provided by NTA).

### Question 4
**What must an SPSV driver have to drive an SPSV?**
- A) Only a valid SPSV driver licence
- B) Only a licensed SPSV vehicle
- C) A valid SPSV driver licence AND a licensed SPSV vehicle
- D) Only a regular driving licence
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: To drive an SPSV, you must have: A valid SPSV driver licence in addition to your regular driving licence, AND a licensed SPSV vehicle.

## Chapter 7: Working as an SPSV Operator (Pages 80-102)

### Question 1
**What must be displayed on the dashboard of an SPSV?**
- A) Insurance certificate
- B) Driver display card
- C) Vehicle registration
- D) Tax clearance certificate
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Your driver display card must be clearly shown on the dashboard whenever you are working as an SPSV driver.

### Question 2
**What must taxi drivers have for cashless payments?**
- A) A mobile phone
- B) An in-taxi point of sale payment device
- C) A credit card reader
- D) A bank account
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Taxi drivers must have an in-taxi point of sale payment device and must accept cashless payments for taxi journeys when requested by customers.

### Question 3
**When can an SPSV driver refuse service?**
- A) For short journeys
- B) For any reason
- C) Only for passengers who smoke, are intoxicated, or exceed capacity
- D) Never
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: You may reasonably refuse service to carry passengers who smoke, act in an aggressive/intoxicated manner, are likely to damage the vehicle, request journeys over 30km in a taxi, or exceed the permitted number of people.

### Question 4
**What must be provided to customers at the end of a taxi journey?**
- A) A business card
- B) A receipt from the taximeter printer
- C) A handwritten note
- D) Nothing
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: A taxi driver must have the ability to print a receipt from the printer attached to the taximeter. They must offer it to the customer at the end of every journey.

## Chapter 13: Preparing for Your Test (Pages 197-207)

### Question 1
**How many modules does the SPSV Driver Entry Test have?**
- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4
**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The SPSV Driver Entry Test has two modules: Industry Knowledge Module and Area Knowledge Module (county-specific).

### Question 2
**What must new SPSV driver licence applicants pass?**
- A) Only the Industry Knowledge module
- B) Only the Area Knowledge module
- C) Both parts of the SPSV Driver Entry Test
- D) Only a practical driving test
**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: If you are a new SPSV driver licence applicant, you must pass the SPSV Driver Entry Test, which has two modules.

### Question 3
**What must be completed after passing the SPSV Driver Entry Test?**
- A) Nothing
- B) Only Safeguarding module
- C) Only Disability Awareness module
- D) Both Safeguarding and Disability Awareness modules
**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Once you pass the SPSV Driver Entry Test, you must then complete the free, online eLearning modules: Safeguarding module and Disability Awareness module.

### Question 4
**When might a driver be required to take the Area Knowledge Test?**
- A) Never, if already licensed
- B) Only when renewing
- C) When wanting to operate in an additional county, or if NTA receives 3+ complaints about area knowledge

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: If you already hold an SPSV driver licence and wish to stand or ply for hire in a county other than where you were originally licensed, you must pass the area knowledge module for the additional area. You may also be asked to sit the test if NTA receives three or more complaints relating to your area knowledge.
